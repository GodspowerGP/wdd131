Why Visit Kyoto?

Ancient Temples

Home to over 1,600 Buddhist temples and 400 Shinto shrines, Kyoto is the historical heart of Japan. Witness stunning architecture and serene zen gardens that have stood for centuries.

Breathtaking Nature

From the iconic Arashiyama Bamboo Grove to the vibrant autumn leaves of Kiyomizu-dera, Kyoto offers unparalleled natural beauty across all four seasons.
